3.5 E. Identifiers and duplicates

A session key represents the pair of the trimmed user_pseudo_id and an integer ga_session_id. The sample export had usable values for both on every row checked. The mart returned zero duplicate session keys, 845 cross-date sessions, and 5,272 sessions with no observed session_start in the fixed window. Cross-date means the pair appeared on more than one exported event date. Window edges can truncate activity. The raw comparison returned zero excess rows sharing a complete user/timestamp/event-name triplet. This diagnostic can miss repeated instrumentation with different timestamps, and identical triplets, when observed, require investigation before changing event counts.

3.6 F. Reconciliation

GA4-5’s prior raw-to-mart reconciliation had zero differences for total events, page_view, and purchase. That validates the sample transformation. Direct numerical reconciliation with GA4 was infeasible because demo property 213025502 was viewed in an October–November 2026 window, whereas sample dates are in 2020–2021 and the underlying event population is unverified. For production, align property and streams, property time zone, processing cutoff, date range, filters, event population, reporting identity, and definitions. Compare GA4 connector event counts, active users where supported, and sessions with scope-aligned BigQuery results. Investigate residual differences using consent, modeling, thresholding, reporting estimates, and late arrivals. GA4-3 reconciliation · GA4 export schema

  2. Use the questionnaire’s logical IDs as dictionary keys. Assign labels only on derived columns. Keep response-status companions for missing, not applicable, refusal, screening termination, and Cannot judge. Numeric zero remains valid only where the item defines it, such as a verified unselected Q2 option on an answered question. Confirm Q2 selection context before converting unchecked to zero. Outside the shown/answered population, leave option values missing and capture routing status.
  3. Derive eligibility, decision focus, confirmed exposure, a two-city grouping, and a timing-band ordered factor using dplyr::mutate(). Keep Undecided and Already chosen as separate timing statuses. Exclude cases with incomplete or unconfirmed exposure from content ratings. 6 Reliability and composite scale readiness

Construct Items Proposed scoring
Information quality IQ1–IQ4 Report clarity, relevance, coverage and contact usefulness individually.
Message credibility MC1–MC3 Candidate mean across three descriptive ratings.
Provider trustworthiness TR1–TR3 Candidate mean across three agreement ratings.
Presentation professionalism PR1–PR3 Provisional candidate mean after wording and measurement review.
Consultation intention CI1–CI3 Candidate mean across three agreement ratings, interpreted as three-month intention toward San Gabriel.

Ratings have ordered 1–5 response categories. All verified items point in the same direction, so no reverse coding is specified. Cannot judge is separate from ordinary nonresponse and never contributes to a mean. The default composite rule requires all three items and rescales nothing. A two-item rule can be tested as a documented sensitivity analysis after approval. Higher means indicate more of the measured attribute. Treat numeric means as approximately interval only with a stated equal-spacing assumption and use an ordinal sensitivity analysis when appropriate.

For authorized data, examine item distributions, complete-case N, inter-item correlations, poorly performing items, floor/ceiling patterns, and Cronbach’s alpha. Review dimensionality and construct separation across candidate groups, using ordinal correlations when sample size and cell counts support them. An isolated one-factor model for three items can be just-identified, so its fit alone cannot establish dimensionality. Release a mean only after item meaning and measurement evidence support it. Keep empirical reliability pending until the approved sample is available.
